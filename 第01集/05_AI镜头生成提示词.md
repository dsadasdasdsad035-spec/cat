# 第一集 AI 镜头生成提示词

## 角色一致性基准
主角猫外观：tiny brown and black tabby cat, amber eyes, cream muzzle, white chest, white paws, natural cat anatomy, realistic fur.
保持同一只猫的脸型、虎斑分布、白爪、胸口颜色和眼睛颜色一致。建议把用户提供的主角猫多角度照片作为图像参考；不要只依赖文字提示词。

## AI-01：雨夜中的小猫（约6秒）
**Prompt**
9:16 vertical cinematic photorealistic animal drama, a tiny vulnerable brown and black tabby kitten with amber eyes, cream muzzle, white chest and white paws, crouching in a sheltered recessed corner beside an urban drainage channel during a rainy night, wet pavement, distant warm streetlights reflected in puddles, low ground-level camera from kitten height, subtle trembling, cautious eye movements, natural fur detail, realistic rain and shadows, shallow depth of field, restrained emotional documentary cinematography, no injury, no blood, no text, no collar.

**Negative prompt（若工具支持）**
cartoon, anime, illustration, extra legs, extra paws, deformed face, changing fur pattern, blue eyes, collar, clothing, blood, wounds, graphic suffering, text, watermark, logo, oversaturated lighting.

## AI-02：第一次遇见人类（约6秒）
**Prompt**
Photorealistic cinematic close-up from the eye level of a tiny brown tabby kitten with white paws and amber eyes. A human crouches at a respectful distance and slowly extends an open hand, palm relaxed and fingers still. The kitten looks at the hand, ears slightly lowered, then cautiously lifts its head. Rainy urban background softly out of focus, realistic anatomy, natural lighting, restrained emotion, no sudden movement, no forced grabbing, vertical 9:16.

**Negative prompt**
cartoon, aggressive grabbing, frightened animal in extreme distress, deformed fingers, extra limbs, changing cat markings, text, watermark, logo.

## AI-03：超能力觉醒伏笔（约5秒）
**Prompt**
Extreme close-up of the same brown and black tabby cat with amber eyes, cream muzzle and white whiskers, resting safely on a soft yellow blanket in a real home. The cat slowly raises its gaze toward a human standing nearby. A very subtle cool blue reflection appears in its pupils, followed by a faint shimmering light that is visible only for a moment. Photorealistic fur, natural cat anatomy, grounded live-action cinematography, warm home lighting, restrained magical realism, no humanoid transformation, no text, vertical 9:16.

**Negative prompt**
laser eyes, glowing full face, cartoon magic, humanoid cat, altered eye color, altered fur pattern, extra eyes, text, watermark, logo.

## AI-04：猫咪主观视角（可选，约5秒）
**Prompt**
Vertical 9:16 photorealistic live-action shot from the low eye-level perspective of a small kitten inside a quiet home. Human legs pass in the background and appear tall from the kitten's perspective. Warm natural window light, subtle handheld camera movement, realistic floor texture, soft shadows, no visible face required, intimate documentary mood, shallow depth of field.

## 生成与后期建议
1. 先用真实猫咪照片建立角色参考图。
2. 每个镜头单独生成，不要一次要求模型完成整集。
3. 先生成静态关键帧确认花纹和构图，再做图生视频。
4. 人手、猫爪、眼睛容易变形，关键镜头应多生成候选版本。
5. 字幕和“能力解锁”等界面文字建议后期添加，避免AI生成乱码。
6. AI镜头的光线、色温和颗粒感尽量贴近实拍素材。
